Privacy policy

Money Tracker for Android · Effective 11 November 2026

Money Tracker keeps your financial records on your phone. There is no Money Tracker server and no account with us: we cannot see your data, and we do not collect, sell or share it. The app has no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter or import — accounts, cards, transactions, budgets, bills, investments and goals — is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Sync through your own Google Drive (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the app stores a copy of your records in the hidden app data area of your own Google Drive, so your other phones signed in to the same account stay up to date and a new phone can restore them. Only Money Tracker can read that area; it does not appear in your Drive and it is not shared with us.

Signing in shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app on your phone, to show which account is in use. To remove the Drive copy: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Money Tracker → Delete hidden app data.

Bank SMS (optional)

If you allow it, the app reads incoming SMS messages to recognise bank and card transaction alerts and record them for you. Messages are read on your phone. For an alert it records, the message text is kept with the transaction so you can see where it came from; like your other records it stays on your phone and, if you sync, in your own Drive. Other messages are ignored and not stored. You can turn this off in Settings, or revoke the SMS permission in Android settings.

Email import (optional)

If you connect a mailbox, the app reads bank emails and statement attachments directly from your mail provider over IMAP, using an app password you create. The password is encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware keystore and never leaves the phone; mailbox settings are not synced. As with SMS, the text behind each transaction it records is kept with that transaction.

Security

You can lock the app with a PIN or your fingerprint. Updates are verified against a published checksum and the app's signing key before they install.

Children

Money Tracker is not directed at children under 13.
